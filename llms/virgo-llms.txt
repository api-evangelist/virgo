# Virgo

> Virgo builds AI foundation models for gastrointestinal (GI) endoscopy, targeting gut-mediated diseases such as inflammatory bowel disease and colorectal cancer. Its flagship model EndoDINO was trained on the largest known endoscopy dataset (130,037 videos / ~3.5 billion frames). Products span EndoDINO (foundation model), EndoML (GI-specific build tooling), Virgo Cloud (endoscopy video capture and management), and Virgo Trials (clinical trial acceleration with automatic data capture and AI workflows).

This file was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public Virgo web surface. Virgo does not currently publish a public developer API, developer portal, or API reference; access to Virgo Cloud is via authenticated login.

## Products
- [EndoDINO](https://virgosvs.com/): Foundation model for GI endoscopy, trained on 130,037 endoscopy videos (~3.5B frames).
- [EndoML](https://virgosvs.com/): GI-specific tooling environment for building clinical and research workflows on top of EndoDINO.
- [Virgo Cloud](https://cloud.virgosvs.com/): Video capture and management platform for GI endoscopy procedures (authenticated login).
- [Virgo Trials](https://virgosvs.com/): Clinical trial acceleration with automatic data capture, endoscopic scoring, placebo-response prediction, subgroup identification, and AI biomarker discovery.
